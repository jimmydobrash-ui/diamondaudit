One standard. One scoreboard. One record.

The same evaluation for every athlete — and something to point to when a decision gets questioned.

Standardize the scoring

Build one template — the skills you care about, scored on a calibrated 0–10 scale with the rubric on screen. Every athlete, judged the same way.

Your whole staff, one number

Coaches evaluate the same players independently. DiamondAudit rolls their scores into one number — and keeps each coach's view when you need to compare.

One results view

A single ranked leaderboard you can filter by age, sort by skill, and export to CSV — instead of ten clipboards and a group text.

A record behind every cut

Every score is saved, per coach, per player. When a decision gets questioned, you have the evaluation to point to.

How it works

Set it up once, then run the whole tryout from your phones.

1

Set your template

Choose the skills you score and the scale you score them on. Catchers, pitchers, position players — calibrate it once.

2

Score every athlete

Your staff evaluates on phones or tablets against the same rubric, then grades each athlete Offer, Bubble, or Pass.

3

Decide with the results

Watch the leaderboard fill in, build your roster, and export the record — defensible the moment someone asks.

Why this exists

Built by a coach who got tired of defending tryout cuts with a stack of clipboards. Tryouts move fast, every coach scores a little differently, and the results are hard to stand behind when a player or parent asks why. DiamondAudit makes the scoring consistent and the outcome a record — so the hard calls hold up.
