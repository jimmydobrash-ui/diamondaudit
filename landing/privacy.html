DiamondAudit is a tool for baseball and softball programs to run and score player tryouts. This policy describes, in plain terms, what we handle and why.

What we collect

Coach accounts. Name and email for the coaches and admins who sign in.

Player & evaluation data. Roster details and scores that coaches enter for the athletes they evaluate. Programs are responsible for the player information they add, which may include minors.

How we use it

To provide the service — letting your staff evaluate players, view results, and manage your organization. We do not sell your data.

Data ownership & deletion

Your organization's data belongs to your program. Contact us to export or delete your organization's data.
