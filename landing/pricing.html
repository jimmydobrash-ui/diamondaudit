Questions

What counts as a "player"?

An athlete on your roster for a tryout. On Per Tryout, you're billed for the players you evaluate at that event — not your whole database.

Can I switch to a club license later?

Yes. Start free or per-tryout, and move to the year-round license whenever the math makes sense for your program.

Is my program's data mine?

Always. Your roster, scores, and results belong to your organization, and you can export the full record to CSV anytime.

How is players' data protected?

Every organization's data is isolated — coaches only ever see their own program's players and evaluations. See our Privacy Policy.

Not sure which fits your program?

Tell us how many athletes you run through tryouts and we'll point you to the right plan.
